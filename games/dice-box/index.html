Dice Box

Five dice in a glass box with a felt floor. Shake the phone and the dice fly; hold it still and they settle where physics puts them.

The box is the phone. Tilting, lifting and twisting all count.

Fairness

Each throw starts with every die showing 1, shakes the box hard for 2 s (random accelerations to 50 m/s², rotations to 16 rad/s), then lets the dice settle. Faces are pooled across dice. A fair engine gives a flat count; χ² has 5 degrees of freedom. A gentle shake is not a fair throw here or on a table: dice that are only slid keep their face.
